# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Kipory serves product, operations, data, and engineering teams responsible for understanding, building, and improving complex business processes.

## Product Purpose

Kipory turns datasets, documents, and media into governed production workflows. It exists to help teams move complex business processes from raw information to usable operational knowledge in days rather than quarters.

## Positioning

Kipory combines deterministic processing and agentic AI flows in one governed platform. Its position is that reliability, intelligence, governance, and delivery belong in the same flow system rather than in separate custom-built tools.

## Operating Context

Teams evaluate Kipory while mapping how work and information move through their business, identifying where workflows stop, and considering how to deliver governed knowledge into the systems where their teams already operate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The platform works across datasets, documents, and media.
- Deterministic and agentic AI flows operate together within one governed system.
- The public product is currently pre-launch and access is managed through a waiting list and direct conversations.
- Pricing and the timing or scope of expanded access remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is Kipory.
- Product language centers on flows, governed knowledge, deterministic processing, agentic AI, and operational movement.
- The voice is direct, technical, and operational without overstating unverified outcomes.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current product and positioning copy: `src/app/page.tsx` and `src/app/layout.tsx`.
- Current access and inquiry flows: `src/app/waitlist/page.tsx` and `src/app/contact/page.tsx`.
- Reusable animated flow demonstrations and their Storybook controls are included in the repository.
- No confirmed customer names, testimonials, case studies, benchmarks, pricing, or third-party validation are currently on hand. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Make complex operational movement understandable.
2. Keep deterministic reliability and agentic intelligence in one governed system.
3. Turn heterogeneous source material into usable knowledge, not another raw-data destination.
4. Earn trust through traceability and precise claims rather than unsupported proof.
5. Help lean cross-functional teams move from idea to production workflow quickly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must preserve keyboard access, semantic page structure, readable contrast, and reduced-motion alternatives for animated flow demonstrations.
